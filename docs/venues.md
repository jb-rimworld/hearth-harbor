# Venues, shops and staffing (ModuleId.ShopsFood, without kitchen and dishes)

Code: `Source/Hearth/Venues` (namespace `Hearth.ShopsFood`; `Venues` is a contract type in `Hearth`, so the namespace cannot be `Hearth.Venues`).
Defs: `Common/Defs/Venues`, `Common/Patches/Venues_DesignationCategory.xml`. Texts: `Keyed/HH_Venues.xml` (generated by `tools/gen_venues_keyed.py`).
Tests: `venues.sign|shop|staff|tavern|vending|market|hidden|wait` in `VenueTests.cs`.

## What a player sees
- **Venue Sign** (free-standing `HH_VenueSign`, wall-mounted `HH_VenueSignWall`): the room of the sign is the business.
  Controls: kind of business, opening hours (presets Day / Evening / Night / Always from settings, hour pickers, 24 h strip), prices (tiers Charity … Luxury and a slider),
  "colony may take goods", rename. Selected: the room is outlined; the inspect text shows hours, who is on duty, price level, stock and a readiness checklist.
- **Kinds** (`VenueTypeDef`): tavern, shop, restaurant (kitchen module), casino (casino module), spa (wellness), lounge (waiting room).
- **Staffing** (Serving work type, work giver `HH_ServeVenue`): during opening hours a colonist goes to the counter (`HH_ShopCounter`, also a till spot) or next to the sign
  and tends it for a shift (default 2 h, Social XP). Venues that need staff (tavern, restaurant, casino, spa; setting) are open only while somebody tends them.
  Venues that do not need staff but have nobody behind the counter run as self-service at -10 %.
- **Waiting and the night bell** (5.13): a guest who wants a staffed service waits (patience setting) or, outside hours, rings the bell. Awake colonists with Serving come;
  the service costs +50 % (minimum 3 silver). Nobody comes: "Rang the bell. Nobody came." and the guest is annoyed. A lounge halves the annoyance and is where guests wait.
- **Shopping** (activity `venues.shop`): everything on stockpile cells and in storage buildings inside a shop room is for sale (market value x sign factor x setting x
  self-service/bell). The guest walks there, browses, pays through `GuestApi.ChargeGuest` (so it lands in the colony till), takes the item home. Price decisions follow KONZEPT 6.1
  (tolerance by wealth, rho thresholds, price thoughts as satisfaction).
- **Tavern** (`venues.drink`): same flow for drinks (liquor / fluid ingestibles), drunk on the spot.
- **Vending machine** (`HH_VendingMachine`, `venues.vending`): powered storage building for food, drugs and medicine; sells to guests at any hour without staff.
- **N45 goods are off limits**: goods for sale are forbidden for colonists (vanilla forbidding, synchronised every 250 ticks; only what we forbade is released again) unless the sign allows it.
- **Big market** (N48): every `venues.marketDays` days (default 15, +-20 %), only while at least one shop exists: several trader caravans and day visitor groups of friendly factions at once,
  one "Market day" letter. Debug action: Hearth & Harbor > Hold market day now.
- **Hidden replaced vanilla furniture** (N49): `VanillaReplacementDef` (XML) or `VanillaHiding.Register(vanilla, replacement)`; the vanilla designator leaves the architect category once the
  replacement is available. Setting `venues.hideVanilla` (global).

## For other modules
- Ask `VenueApi.Gate(venue)` / `VenueApi.IsOpenForBusiness(venue)` before serving: it includes hours, staff, the night bell. `Venue.IsOpenNow` from the contract is hours only.
- `VenueApi.NeedsStaff`, `IsStaffed`, `Staffer`, `PostCell`, `CounterOf`, `PriceMultiplier`, `PriceOf`, `VenueOfSign`.
- A `VenueTypeDef` may carry `<modExtensions><li Class="Hearth.ShopsFood.VenueSaleExtension"><sells>Goods|Drinks|None</sells></li></modExtensions>`; only selling types get their room's goods forbidden.
- `GuestFallback` drives idle vanilla visitors through `GuestActivities.TryPick` while the Inn module is off. Set `GuestFallback.Enabled = false` if another module already does that.
- Wall-mounted signs: `Venues.RoomOfSign` in the contract sends attachments to the wall cell (no room); `Patch_RoomOfSign_Attachment` repairs that until the contract is fixed.
