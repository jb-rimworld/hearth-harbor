### Top 12 Crash, Performance, Mod-Compatibility & Save/Removal Risks (Ordered by Importance)

#### 1. `ForbidUtility.InAllowedArea` hot-path patch does room and venue-list lookups per cell
- **File/Def**: [GuestPatches.cs](file:///Users/julius/Documents/Coding/22_RimWorld/HearthHarbor/Source/Hearth/Guests/GuestPatches.cs#L61-L70) (`Patch_ForbidUtility_InAllowedArea`) & [GuestWings.cs](file:///Users/julius/Documents/Coding/22_RimWorld/HearthHarbor/Source/Hearth/Guests/GuestWings.cs#L159-L176) (`GuestWings.AllowedFor`).
- **Problem**: `AGENTS.md` Rule 2 warns against per-cell pathing/forbid hot paths without a cheap early-out. While colonists early-out via `GuestRegistry.IsWingRestricted(forPawn)` (`HashSet<int>`), for every checked-in guest `InAllowedArea` is called hundreds of times per job search (joy scans, `RCellFinder`, Dubs Bad Hygiene fixture searches, VEF pathing). Whenever cell `c` is outside `wing[c]`, `AllowedFor` calls `c.GetRoom(p.Map)` and loops through `Venues.All(p.Map)` ([Venues.cs](file:///Users/julius/Documents/Coding/22_RimWorld/HearthHarbor/Source/Hearth/Contracts/Venues.cs#L182-L202)), which validates `StillValid` and can rebuild/sort the map's venue list inside a per-cell hot path.
- **Concrete fix**: Maintain a per-map `BoolGrid openVenueCells` on `MapComponent_HH_GuestWings` refreshed every 250 ticks (or when `Venues.MarkDirty` runs), so `AllowedFor` is two `O(1)` array index checks: `wing[c] || comp.OpenVenueCells[c]`.

#### 2. `RestUtility.IsValidBedFor` calls `RestUtility.CanUseBedNow` on a hot path without checking `!__result` or `sleeper == null`
- **File/Def**: [GuestPatches.cs](file:///Users/julius/Documents/Coding/22_RimWorld/HearthHarbor/Source/Hearth/Guests/GuestPatches.cs#L33-L46) (`Patch_RestUtility_IsValidBedFor.Postfix`).
- **Problem**: When `isGuest` is true, line 43 sets `__result = guestBed && (GuestRegistry.Instance?.Get(sleeper)?.Bed == bedThing) && RestUtility.CanUseBedNow(bedThing, sleeper, false);` even if vanilla `__result` was already `false`. In vanilla and mods like Dubs Bad Hygiene or Hospitality add-ons, `CanUseBedNow` and `IsValidBedFor` call each other depending on call site, risking infinite recursion or redundant reachability/reservation checks. Also, `sleeper` is not null-checked before `GuestRegistry.IsRegisteredGuest(sleeper)`.
- **Concrete fix**: Start `Postfix` with `if (!__result || sleeper == null || !Modules.On(ModuleId.Inn)) return;`. Remove the `RestUtility.CanUseBedNow` call from line 43 (`__result` from vanilla already validated the bed; just check `if (isGuest) __result = guestBed && GuestRegistry.Instance?.Get(sleeper)?.Bed == bedThing; else if (guestBed) __result = false;`).
