# Credits

Hearth & Harbor is written from scratch under the MIT license. No code, textures or texts of the mods below were copied.
Many of their ideas, though, are re-imagined here, and the mod would not exist without them.

## Inspiration

- **Orion**: Hospitality, Gastronomy, Cash Register and Therapy, the mods that taught RimWorld how to be a host.
- **Zaljerem, jjanow and mrbeverage**: Hospitality (Continued), Gastronomy (Continued) and Cash Register (Continued), which kept
  them alive for later versions.
- **Adamas**: Storefront, Casino, Spa, Vending Machines and Hospital.
- **ledronas**: Room Service.
- **RobTheRobotGuard**: Hotels and Recreation.
- **gatoviejo**: Invite to Stay.
- **ferny** and **Cruesoe**: Progression: Hospitality and ProgressionLESS: Hospitality.

## Tools and libraries

- [Harmony](https://github.com/pardeike/Harmony) by Andreas Pardeike (brrainz), the only dependency.
- [Krafs.Rimworld.Ref](https://github.com/krafs/RimRef) reference assemblies for the 1.5 and 1.6 builds.
- Textures were drawn as SVG and rendered with [resvg](https://github.com/linebender/resvg).

## Music

- *Harbor Morning*, *Lanterns at the Inn* and *Snow on the Sign* (Common/Sounds/HH/Music) were generated for Hearth & Harbor with
  ElevenLabs Music (instrumental, no vocals) and are shipped with the mod. They join the normal playlist and can be switched off in the
  mod settings (General → Music).

## Translations

The ten translations were made with AI help. Corrections from native speakers are very welcome: open an issue or a pull request
with changes to `Common/Languages/<language>/`.

## Built with
